# The Problem: Driver Fatigue Kills

- **The Hidden Hazard**: Drowsiness causes over **20% of all commercial vehicle crashes** worldwide.
- **Split-Second Risk**: A 2-second micro-sleep at 60 mph means traveling **175 feet completely blind**.
- **Connectivity Gaps**: Highways, tunnels, and rural routes frequently lack stable 4G/5G cellular signals.
- **Cloud Latency is Too Slow**: Waiting for cloud servers to analyze video frames takes **300ms to 1 second**—far too late to prevent an accident.

> **Key Challenge**: We need an instant, offline, and reliable safety system inside every vehicle.

# How It Works: Facial Behavioral Analysis

The system continuously analyzes key visual indicators to grade driver alertness in real time:

- **Eye Monitoring**: Measures eye openness, blink duration, and percentage of eye closure over time.
- **Yawn Detection**: Detects mouth opening frequency and sustained yawning duration.
- **Head Pose & Nodding**: Identifies downward head drops and prolonged off-road distraction.
- **Automatic Driver Calibration**: Learns each driver's natural baseline in under 10 seconds without manual setup.

# Multi-Tier Safety Alert System

- **Level 0: Safe Driving** — Normal eye and head activity. Continuous background monitoring.
- **Level 1: Mild Fatigue** — Slight drop in eye openness. Silent visual warning on dashboard.
- **Level 2: Moderate Drowsiness** — Sustained blinks or yawning. Soft chime + rest break recommendation.
- **Level 3: Critical Hazard** — Micro-sleep detected or head nodding. **Loud auditory alarm + seat vibration**.
